Layout: stylised world map with countries highlighted in signature colours, name-labelled, with small dimmed leader icons at their location.

Active event brings up two large portrait popups — actor (left, over their region) and receiver (right) — each with a speech-bubble line. Missile arcs from actor's country to receiver's country with 🚀 mid-flight. Receiver gets red dashed glow ring; actor gets green firing pulse.

Khameneverhere never appears — his portrait is the empty-chair tableau (cycles between empty-chair / hospital-leg / cardboard-cutout each round). His bubble still pops up, faxed-in.

Damage badge floats over receiver's country, tilted comic-book style.

Pacing: each event ~1.8s — green pulse on actor → missile launch → arc → impact + red ring + damage badge → portraits and bubbles materialise → bubbles dwell ~0.6s → all fade → next event. Fast Resolve = 3× speed.

How the map handles other phases:

  • Defence intercepts: 🛡 burst on the arc; missile fades before reaching target; receiver's portrait shows relief; no damage badge.
  • Defence builds: 🛡 icon pops up over the building country; no arc.
  • Factory builds: ⚙ icon pops up; pop counter ticks up next round.
  • Propaganda: paper-aeroplane / pamphlet trail arcs from sender to receiver; receiver's pop counter ticks down.
  • Wooing: heart / handshake icon arcs gently between two countries with a brief flash of mutual portraits.
  • Final Retaliation: eliminated country's icon goes black, starburst of arcs fires from it to all grudge targets simultaneously.
  • Multi-impact (3 missiles converging on one target): all arrive in roughly one beat; receiver's portrait stays up across all impacts; damage badges stack and pulse.
