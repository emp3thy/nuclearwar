Sequence in motion: each beat is ~1.3 seconds — portrait shifts in, quote types out, state-change badge slides in, beat ends. Total ~8 seconds for 6 leaders. Skip jumps to Round Summary.

State-change badges: red (harm), green (gain), brown (neutral state shift). They carry the mechanical impact in plain language so the player learns the rules through narrative.

Khameneverhere's portrait is always "empty" — the cardboard cutout / hospital tableau / empty chair cycles each round. His "voice" is faxed/printed.

Eliminated leaders get a single dramatic beat at the end of Resolution (not in Reactions) — body slumps, then their warheads launch (Final Retaliation). Reactions only shows surviving leaders.

Reduced motion setting: all 6 cards appear at once with no animation, auto-advances after 4 seconds.
